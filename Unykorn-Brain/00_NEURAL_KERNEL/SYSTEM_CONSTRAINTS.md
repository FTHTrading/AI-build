# SYSTEM BOUNDARIES & OPERATIONAL GUARDS

1. **Gateway Boundary**:
   - Unykorn LLC is an **infrastructure and technology rails provider**.
   - Unykorn LLC is **NOT** a direct lender, broker-dealer, or qualified custodian.
   - All settlements, escrows, and custody operations must connect to external [[CUSTODY_GATEWAYS]] and qualified institutional partners.

2. **Entity Strictness**:
   - All software, repositories, API surfaces, and contracts exist solely under **Unykorn LLC**.
   - Deprecated or legacy entities (e.g., historical payment branding or legacy LLC variants) are completely purged from all active builds.
